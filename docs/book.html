About these pictures

Every image in this book was drawn by Vacant Sunlight, a software renderer written from scratch for this project in plain JavaScript. It uses no libraries, textures, photographs or 3D models. Seed 1981 grows the house, the pool and the palms from a handful of rules, which is why every page shows exactly the same place. The sun follows its real path over 34° north in late July. Shadows are traced exactly through a bounding volume hierarchy, the pool is refracted and reflected, and every pixel is the average of nine samples.

The style is a homage to the flat, sunlit poolside paintings of Hiroshi Nagai, made without borrowing any of his images.
