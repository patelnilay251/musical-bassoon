One Day in Paloma Bay

One summer day in a seaside town that doesn't exist, from before dawn to the last room going. The film passes through every postcard in the book, each at its own minute, and follows a visitor who is never seen: only their yellow convertible, the song on its radio, a towel on the sand, a room light coming on. Every picture, sound and note is made from rules, by the same engine as the town. The 1080p master is on GitHub.
